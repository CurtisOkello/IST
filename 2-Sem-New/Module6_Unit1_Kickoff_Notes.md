# Module 6, Unit 1 Kickoff Notes
**Today's session, 1:15-1:25 PM segment — 10 minutes, right before wrap-up**

Purpose: this isn't the full lesson (that starts tomorrow) — it's a short teaser to open the door into Module 6 and get them mentally checked in for tomorrow's deep dive.

---

### 1. The pivot (1-2 min)
Bridge from what they just reviewed to what's next:
> "Everything we just recalled — CIA Triad, threats vs. vulnerabilities, IDS/IPS, OWASP — was about *understanding* security. Module 6 is where you start *actively testing* it. This is the semester where you stop being a student of security and start acting like the people who get paid to break things on purpose — legally."

### 2. Frame the big question (2-3 min)
Pose it and let it sit rather than answering it fully:
> "If I handed you a company's IP range right now and said 'find the weak points,' where would you even start? And once you found ten weaknesses, how would you know which one to fix first?"

That question *is* Unit 1 — Vulnerability Assessment is the discipline of answering both halves: finding weaknesses systematically, then prioritizing them so remediation effort goes where it matters most.

### 3. Roadmap the unit (3-4 min)
Quick whiteboard/verbal outline of where the next 4 sessions go — don't teach the content, just name it so it's not a surprise:
- **Tomorrow (Theory):** What vulnerability management actually is as an ongoing lifecycle — and the three words people constantly confuse: *vulnerability, exploit, risk*.
- **Thu (Deep Dive):** How you find vulnerabilities in the first place — reconnaissance and fingerprinting, from the attacker's side and the defender's side.
- **Fri (Case Study):** CVSS — how a vulnerability gets a number, and why that number alone doesn't tell you what to fix first.
- **Mon (Lab):** Hands-on — running a real scanner (Nessus/OpenVAS) against a lab target and producing an actual findings report.

### 4. One grounding definition to leave them with (1-2 min)
Give them a single clean sentence to walk out with, so tomorrow doesn't start cold:
> "Vulnerability Assessment is the systematic process of discovering, classifying, and prioritizing security weaknesses in a system — before someone else finds them for you."

### 5. Close (30 sec)
> "Come tomorrow having thought about that opening question. We'll build the real answer together."
